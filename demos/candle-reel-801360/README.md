# Candle reel concept — 99Freelas 801360

Public concept for the official listing [Criação de vídeo com IA para loja](https://www.99freelas.com.br/project/criacao-de-video-com-ia-para-loja-801360?fs=t).

## What this proves

- A short vertical 9:16 social-video direction with three AI-generated generic product scenes.
- Slow zoom, dissolves, concise title cards and an MP4 player that works on mobile.
- A reproducible local render script: `python tools/render-candle-reel.py` (requires FFmpeg).

## What it does not claim

This is an independent visual study, not the client's video or candle product. The client says they already have a model and want modifications; the source project, footage, exact edit instructions, brand assets, audio requirements and AI policy were not supplied. The demo is silent and cannot confirm compatibility with that source. The final scope should adapt the client's actual template and approved materials.

## Design and accessibility

- Responsive layout with a 9:16 native video element and keyboard-accessible playback controls.
- Reduced-motion preference is respected on page scrolling; the video is user-controlled and does not autoplay.
- Contrast, text hierarchy and labels were reviewed using the local `frontend-ui-engineering` skill.

## Generated assets

The three original scenes were generated for this generic concept. No real product, logo, customer or performance metric is shown. Replace them with the client's authorized material only after the project is awarded.
